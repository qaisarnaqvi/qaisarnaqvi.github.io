<h1>Research Contributions</h1>
<h4>Innovations in Electromagnetics</h4>
</div>

<div class="research-intro">
<span class="drop-cap">P</span>rofessor Qaisar Abbas Naqvi is a leading researcher on the <b>Kobayashi Potential Method (KPM)</b> in Electromagnetics. 
<br><br>
Our group has successfully applied KPM to classical geometries (strip, slit, disk, hole) and developed mathematical models for scattering in complex media including Lorentz-Drude, chiral, and topological insulator mediums.

<h3>Kobayashi Potential Method</h3>
<p>A rigorous mathematical technique for mixed boundary value problems. We have extended KPM to analyze scattering from classical geometries like strips, slits, and disks in complex non-integer dimensional spaces.</p>
</div>

<h3>Metamaterials & Optics</h3>
<p>Studying metamaterials as a platform for field manipulation. Our models describe propagation in <b>Lorentz-Drude</b>, <b>Chiral</b>, and <b>Topological Insulator</b> media, offering applications in both civil and military domains.</p>
</div>

<h3>Tunable Optical Shifts</h3>
<p>Exploring the <b>Goos-Hänchen (GH)</b> shift in Graphene and Epsilon-Near-Zero (ENZ) slabs. Our studies show these shifts can be actively controlled, aiding the design of advanced photonic sensors and switches.</p>
</div>

<h3>Ghost Waves</h3>
<p>Pioneering work on non-uniform electromagnetic waves that combine characteristics of both propagating and evanescent waves. These "Ghost Waves" open new avenues in dielectric magnetic anisotropic materials.</p>
