## Innovative Contributions
**Prof. Qaisar Abbas Naqvi** is a leading researcher on the **Kobayashi Potential Method (KPM)** in Electromagnetics. He has successfully applied KPM to classical geometries (strip, slit, disk, hole) and developed mathematical models for scattering in the presence of metamaterials (Lorentz-Drude medium, chiral medium, topological insulator medium, and perfect electromagnetic conductor) and/or non-integer dimensional spaces. 

These contributions have been widely cited and have potential applications in both civil and military domains.
