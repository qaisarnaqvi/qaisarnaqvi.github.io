We are proud to announce that **Prof. Dr. Qaisar Abbas Naqvi** has been awarded the prestigious **PAS Gold Medal** in the field of Engineering Sciences.

This award recognizes his lifetime contributions to:
1.  **Fractional Electromagnetics**
2.  **Theoretical Analysis of Metamaterials**
3.  **High-Frequency Scattering Techniques**

The Pakistan Academy of Sciences (PAS) awards this medal to scientists who have made outstanding contributions to research and development.
