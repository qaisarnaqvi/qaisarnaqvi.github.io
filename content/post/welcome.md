## Welcome
Welcome to the research group of **Prof. Dr. Qaisar Abbas Naqvi**, Tenured Professor at Quaid-i-Azam University.

### Latest News
* **Nov 2024:** Delivered an invited talk at the **Shanghai Conference on Solar Energy**.
