**Prof. Dr. Q.A. Naqvi** has been honored with the **SPIE Diversity Outreach Award**. 

SPIE (The International Society for Optics and Photonics) grants this award to educators and researchers who have demonstrated a strong commitment to promoting diversity and inclusion in the field of optics.
