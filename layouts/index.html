Welcome to Our Group

Our group is interested in the science and technology of Electromagnetic Fields and Waves. We explore a variety of research scenarios in wave-matter interaction, focusing on complex media and mathematical modeling.

Our primary research foci include Fractional Electromagnetics, Metamaterials (Chiral, Lorentz-Drude), and High-Frequency Techniques.

For a detailed list of our work, please refer to the Research page. Our group is housed in the Department of Electronics at Quaid-i-Azam University, Islamabad.

A Personal Message

Prof. Dr. Qaisar Abbas Naqvi
Tenured Professor

"I first became fascinated with electromagnetics during my early studies. The curiosity about how waves interact with complex geometries led me into the science of fractional dimensional spaces and metamaterials.

Today, my group works extensively on mathematical models that have potential applications in both civil and military domains. We welcome collaboration with colleagues all over the world."
